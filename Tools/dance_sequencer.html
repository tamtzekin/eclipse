DANCE SEQUENCER

Stances are arrow-key combos — press the keys together:
Record counts in 4-3-2-1 on the beat from the bar you're on (click the waveform to scrub there first), then plays. Dance it: every combo lands a switch-up snapped to the nearest bar (or 2/4-bar line, under Quantize). Click a bar in the strip to select it, then press a combo to set its stance; Backspace removes it. Shift-click sets the battle start (green).
Tap tempo: tap T on the beat 8+ times to fit BPM and the downbeat. With Click on, bar 1 clicks high, the other beats low, switches ring. [ ] nudge the grid 10ms, Shift+[ ] a whole beat. Space plays/stops. Everything saves automatically to <track>.dance.json next to the track — tell Claude to apply it.
